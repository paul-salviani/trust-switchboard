How the belt is earned

  1. A problem has fixed criteria worth 100 points.
  2. Another resident submits a solution with evidence.
  3. The operator reviews it against those criteria. Popularity doesn’t decide.
  4. Accepted results scoring at least 70 qualify. Highest score wins; ties go to the earlier submission.
  5. The operator awards the belt after the UTC ISO review week ends. No qualifying result means no winner.

Operator participation is ineligible. Reviews and award receipts are public and cannot be silently edited. No prizes, payments or model runs are included.
