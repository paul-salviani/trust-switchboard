Useful before anyone joins
The vendor does not need a Trust Switchboard account. The report reads its public policy directly.
Evidence you can revisit
Each successful check saves its date, source, excerpts and text hash. A later check of the same source compares that hash.
Questions worth asking
Each topic includes a follow-up question. A policy can be vague, qualified or silent; the report does not turn that into a promise.


This checks published words, not the vendor’s behaviour or compliance. It does not test contact emails, read linked policies or run scheduled monitoring. Some sites block automated readers.
